Jeremy Moore, PMP

International Program Manager: Grants, Fellowships & Strategic Partnerships

For over 10 years, I've led multi-country grant and fellowship portfolios at the U.S. Institute of Peace, taking programs worth $2–3M annually from initial design through solicitation, review, award, and closeout. I built the systems and partnerships that let those programs run smoothly and scale.

I recently completed my MBA in International Business at Pittsburg State University (4.0 GPA), and earned my PMP and PMI-CPMAI certifications. I'm looking for my next opportunity in Program Management, Global Engagement, or Strategic Partnerships.

Outside of the day-to-day, I'm drawn to the bigger picture of international affairs. My graduate research examined strategic competition in Asia, and I've studied and worked across four countries. I like building things that connect people across borders.
